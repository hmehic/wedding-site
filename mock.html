How it started

Our story

It started at Olive Garden, when we were 16 and 17. We showed up expecting to earn a paycheck. Instead we accidentally met the person we'd spend the rest of our lives with.

Who knew unlimited breadsticks would come with a soulmate?

Life has changed a lot since we met as teenagers, but one thing never did — we've always been each other's favorite person to laugh with. We've cheered each other on, laughed through the chaos, and learned that life is a lot more fun when your best friend is along for the ride.

After eight years of adventures, countless memories, and asking each other "what's for dinner?" a few thousand times, we got engaged. We're so excited to finally make it official and start our next chapter.

Thank you for being here to celebrate with us. We can't wait to eat, dance, and make more unforgettable memories with all of our favorite people.
