FAQs

What is the dress code?

We'd love for our guests to dress in dressy-casual attire. We suggest dresses or jumpsuits for women and dress pants with a button-down shirt for men. Both the ceremony and reception will be outside. Please, no jeans and no hats. We also kindly ask that guests leave white and ivory to the bride.

Can I bring a plus one?

To ensure we can accommodate all our beloved guests, only those explicitly named on your invitation are included. Thank you for understanding!

Are kids welcome?

We've decided to keep the reception as an adults-only celebration, allowing all our guests to enjoy an evening together without children present.

Yes! Please refer to the lodging tab for recommendations.

Yes, the venue can hold about 70–80 vehicles.
