Our story

It all started at Olive Garden when we were just 16 and 17 years old. We showed up expecting to earn a paycheck... but instead, we accidentally met the person we'd spend the rest of our lives with. Who knew unlimited breadsticks would come with a soulmate?

Life has changed a lot since we first met as teenagers, but one thing never did; we've always been each other's favorite person to laugh with. We've cheered each other on, laughed through the chaos, and learned that life is a lot more fun when your best friend is along for the ride.

Somewhere along the way, we also collected three cats, who are fully convinced they own the house and are only allowing us to get married because someone has to keep buying the treats.

After eight years of adventures, countless memories, and probably asking each other, "What's for dinner?" a few thousand times, we got engaged! We're so excited to finally make it official and start our next chapter together.

Thank you for being here to celebrate with us. We can't wait to eat, dance, and make more unforgettable memories with all of our favorite people!
